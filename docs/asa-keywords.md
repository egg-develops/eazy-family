## Why the app isn't appearing for "Familienkalender" searches

Two reasons, both fixable:

- **Paid:** DE/AT campaign is paused. Activate it and the app shows as a sponsored result immediately for every keyword in the ad groups.
- **Organic:** New apps with 0 ratings rank near zero for competitive terms regardless of keywords. Organic rank builds with downloads + ratings over 4–8 weeks. ASA is the only lever right now.

## How to use this file

- **Match type:** Brand + Competitor + Core → **Exact** (high intent, cost control). Add ONE separate **Broad/Discovery** ad group seeded with the Core list to mine new terms cheaply, then promote winners to Exact and push losers into negatives.
- **Negatives:** add at **campaign level**. Use **Broad** match for junk *categories* (games, wallpaper), **Exact** for specific unrelated brands (e.g. `family guy`).
- **Do NOT negate "free / kostenlos / gratis / gratuit"** — the app is free-to-download (freemium), so those are relevant, not junk.
- **`Bring`** (the big Swiss/EU shopping-list app) is included as a competitor in DE/IT/FR because CH is a target market.
- **Bid guidance:** start Exact core/brand at a modest CPT cap and let ASA's suggested bid guide you; competitor terms often need higher bids. Previous US/UK data showed ~€4.76 CPT was overpaying — cap around €2–3 for core, €3–4 for competitor terms, and monitor CPA (target ≤€8–12 per install given the free trial).
- **Broad/Discovery ad group:** add one per campaign seeded with the Core list. Set a lower CPT cap than Exact (≈50% of core CPT). Check weekly — promote any term with ≥3 taps + TTR >5% to the Exact ad group and add zero-convert terms to negatives.
- Lists are comma-separated for direct paste into the ASA keyword box.

## Cross-campaign notes

- **Country is locked after campaign creation** in ASA — to cover DE + AT + CH you create ONE campaign with all three countries selected at creation (you can't add a country later).
- **Customer type "New users" is fine** — it's not what throttles delivery.
- **Localized screenshots:** 1.0.2 (live since Aug 3) carries the de/fr/it screenshots — DE/FR/IT campaigns can go live now.
- **Zero-rating organic rank:** A new app with 0 ratings ranks near the bottom organically even for exact-match keywords. ASA paid placement is the only lever until ratings accumulate (expect 4–8 weeks of campaigns before organic rank builds meaningfully).
- **App name optimization (requires 1.0.3 version):** The non-EN app names are bare "Eazy.Family" (11 chars), burning 19 chars of the highest-weight ASO field. Proposed names that stay under 30 chars:
  - de-DE: `Eazy.Family – Familienplaner` (28 chars)

  - es-ES: `Eazy.Family – Agenda Familiar` (29 chars)
  - pt-PT: `Eazy.Family – Organizador` (25 chars)
